Easy Tomato Pasta

A simple and comforting tomato pasta that is perfect for a quick lunch or dinner.

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Serves: 2 people

Ingredients

  • 200 grams of pasta
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh basil
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil a pot of water and cook the pasta.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a pan.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for about one minute.
  4. Add the tomatoes, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Cook the sauce for about ten minutes.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
  7. Serve with fresh basil and Parmesan cheese.
